About Background Remover

Purpose

Background Remover is a focused, high-precision utility designed to isolate subjects and remove backgrounds cleanly without unnecessary complexity.

100% Privacy Guarantee

Images uploaded to Background Remover are processed directly in-memory and are never stored permanently on disk or used for training. Once your download is complete, the memory buffer is wiped.

Supported Formats & Quality

Accepts JPG, JPEG, PNG, and WEBP formats up to 15MB. Output is delivered as an uncompressed transparent PNG preserving foreground colors, contrast, and edge fidelity.
